# Photos of the series

Put real photographs from the MotoStudent competition here, then list them in `data/site.ts`
under `photos`. The band appears automatically once the list has entries.

Where to get them
- The organiser (Moto Engineering Foundation) publishes press images of each edition. Ask their
  communication team for the press kit and their usage rules. Registered teams usually get access.
- Other teams' photos are theirs: ask before using and credit them.
- Once you have your own build and track photos, they go here too.

Rules
- Credit every photo (the `credit` field): "Photo: MotoStudent / MEF" or the photographer's name.
- Keep files under ~400 KB: 1600 px wide JPEG, quality 80.
- Do not use screenshots from video or images pulled off social media without permission.
